Simple Interest Calculator

A simple calculator that computes the simple interest based on the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period.

Formula

Simple Interest (SI) = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

P = Principal amount

R = Annual rate of interest

T = Time period in years

Example

If:

Principal = ₹10,000

Rate = 5% per year

Time = 2 years

Then:

SI = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = ₹1,000

Total Amount = ₹11,000

Purpose

This project demonstrates the basic calculation of simple interest and can be used as a beginner-friendly programming exercise.
